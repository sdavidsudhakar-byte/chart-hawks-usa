The hawk rises high and looks across the whole sky — checking which hunting grounds have been strong all season before deciding where to hunt next
Sector Lens reads the whole market before you pick a stock. It ranks every NSE sector index across three time horizons — long-term season strength, mid-term trend, and short-term momentum — so you commit capital only where the ground has already proven itself at multiple timeframes.
Column Reference
①  Score History
Long-Term (30W) · Mid-Term (30D) · Short-Term (30D)
Three rows of rank bars in a single card — each bar is one period's percentile score colored green (strong) to red (weak).

LT row — 30 weekly bars. Shows the full season's rank history. Sustained green = confirmed institutional presence over months.
Mid row — 30 daily bars (55-day window). Shows the last month of mid-term momentum. Use to confirm the sector is still in trend, not just coasting.
ST row — 30 daily bars (21-day window). The fastest strip. Spots acceleration or early fades within the longer trend. Hover any bar to see the overlay chart for that day.
②  Long-Term Current Score
Rank 1–99 within group · label = Leader / Improving / Weakening / Lagging
Weekly percentile rank vs all sector peers, based on the full annual price history. Shows where this sector has stood across the whole season — not just recent weeks. A high LT score means institutional money has consistently backed this sector for months. Use ≥ 70 as your minimum bar before committing capital.
③  Mid-Term Current Score
Rank 1–99 within group · 55-day momentum window
Daily percentile rank based on 55-day price momentum vs peers, as of today. Confirms the sector is still actively outperforming — not just living on past glory. When LT and Mid are both high, the sector has both a proven history and ongoing strength.
④  Short-Term Current Score
Rank 1–99 within group · 21-day momentum window
Daily percentile rank based on 21-day price momentum vs peers. Captures where money has rotated in the last month. Use alongside Mid-Term — when both are high, the sector is established and still accelerating. A rising ST on a lagging LT sector can signal early rotation worth watching.
⑤  % Returns — Current Week
Mon open → today's close, current week only
Price return from the last close before this Monday to today's close — always the running week, not trailing 5 days. Useful for spotting which sectors are leading or lagging within the current week's price action. Resets every Monday.
⑥  Weekly Price Chart
52-week candlestick · EMA20 · EMA50 · SMA200
52-week weekly candlestick chart for the sector index. Use it to confirm price structure matches the score — rising EMAs, price above SMA200, and clean candle bodies = healthy trend. Hover to see full OHLCV overlay with RS line.
⑦  % Returns — Current Day
Today's intraday price change
Today's price return vs previous close. A quick pulse check — use it to see which sectors are moving on the current session. High-scoring sectors with positive day returns confirm active participation, not just stale rankings.
⑧  Daily Price Chart
52-bar daily candlestick · EMA20 · EMA50 · SMA200
52-bar daily candlestick chart. Zoom-in view of the recent daily price structure. Use alongside the weekly chart — weekly confirms the bigger trend, daily confirms the entry timing. Hover for full OHLCV overlay.
High-conviction entry
LT Score ≥ 70 · Mid Score ≥ 60 · ST Score ≥ 50 · Current Week return positive
Watch — rotation starting
LT Score any · Mid Score rising · ST Score ≥ 60 · Weekly chart breaking above EMA50
Avoid
LT Score < 40 · Mid + ST both falling · Price below SMA200 on weekly chart
Filter Presets
All three presets read Long-Term Current Score and Mid-Term Current Score (columns ② and ③), plus Ignition also reads Short-Term Current Score (column ④) — the live percentile ranks shown in the table, not the history strip bars. Click a preset button to apply it; click it again to clear it. Established and Emerging are mutually exclusive band-checks that stack into one combined score range, while Ignition is a fully independent toggle that layers on top of either — so you can have Established + Ignition active together, filtering to sectors that are both proven and currently igniting. Activating any preset auto-locks and closes the LT / Mid / ST manual dropdown filters (they grey out) since the preset is now driving the filter directly; use ✕ Clear to unlock them and return to manual band selection.
Established
Sectors with a Leaders-band score (≥ 80) on either the long-term season or the mid-term trend — only one of the two needs to clear the bar, not both. This is the widest "proven strength" screen: a sector that has been the top decile performer over the full annual season, or is currently the top decile performer over the last 55 trading days, passes.

Rule: Long-Term Score ≥ 80 OR Mid-Term Score ≥ 80
Short-Term Score: not checked — ignored either way

Combine with Emerging to widen the floor down to 60, covering the full 60–100 "qualified" range on either horizon. Combine with Ignition to additionally require (via OR, not AND) sectors currently accelerating.
Emerging
Sectors in the Improving band (60–79, just below Leaders) on either the long-term or mid-term score. Catches sectors that are trending toward Leader status but haven't crossed 80 yet — the "building conviction, not yet proven" tier. Same either/or logic as Established, just one band lower.

Rule: Long-Term Score 60–79 OR Mid-Term Score 60–79
Short-Term Score: not checked — ignored either way

Combine with Established (both buttons active at once) to see the full 60–100 range in one view — this is the recommended default for most scanning, since it surfaces both proven leaders and sectors about to join them.
Ignition
Sectors with a Leaders-band Short-Term Score (≥ 80) — pure 21-day momentum, with no requirement on the long-term or mid-term score at all. This is the only preset that reads the ST column; it exists to catch fresh rotation into a sector even if it has no season history or mid-term trend yet.

Rule: Short-Term Score ≥ 80
Long-Term / Mid-Term Score: not checked — a sector can qualify here even while Lagging on both

Combinable with either Established or Emerging (or both) as an OR — e.g. Established + Ignition shows every sector that is either proven (LT/Mid ≥ 80) or currently igniting (ST ≥ 80), not the intersection of both.

🔍 Smart Search Bar

One box that searches everything. Two ways to use it:

1 · Just type a keyword

Type any word and it scans all columns at once — symbol, company, macro, sector, industry, basic industry, and every index the stock belongs to. Multiple words are combined with AND (each word must match somewhere).

2 · Target one field with field:value

Prefix your text to search a single column:

sym:Symbolsym:tcs
co:Company nameco:tata
macro:Macro groupmacro:financial
sector:Sectorsector:"information technology"
industry:Industryindustry:banks
bi:Basic industrybi:refineries
index:Any index membershipindex:"S&P 500"
Multi-word values need quotes: index:"S&P 500" ✔ — without quotes only the first word is used.
🧠 Index membership matching

When you type a plain keyword, the search doesn't just look at the obvious columns — it also checks index membership (the index_list column, currently just "S&P 500" for constituents of that index).

  • Type s&p or 500 → finds every S&P 500 constituent, even though "S&P 500" isn't its own visible column elsewhere.
Two kinds of hit:
• Direct text match — your word literally appears in a visible cell (symbol / company / sector …).
• Index match — your word matched the stock's index membership. Expand the Indices column (the +N chip) to see why a row appeared.
🎯 Exact vs. Contains

Use : for "contains" and = for "exactly equals".

index:"S&P 500"Contains → matches any index name containing "S&P 500"
index="S&P 500"Exactly "S&P 500" → current constituents only
⌨️ Autocomplete

As you type, a dropdown suggests field names and real values (matching index names, sectors, symbols…). Use ↑ / ↓ to move, Enter to pick, Esc to close.

📊 Per-column boxes

The small input under each table header filters just that column — spreadsheet style. They combine with the smart bar and the dropdowns (everything is AND-ed together). Active boxes turn blue.

🔽 Classification dropdowns

Macro → Sector → Industry → Basic Industry. They cascade: picking a sector narrows the industry list to only what's valid, so you can't build an empty combination.

🏷️ Index group buttons
Broad MarketS&P 500, Nasdaq 100, Dow Jones, Russell 2000 (SPY/QQQ/DIA/IWM).
SectoralThe 11 GICS sectors, via their Sector SPDR ETF (XLK, XLF, XLE, XLV…).
Industry~24 real industry ETFs one level deeper than sector (Biotech, Semiconductors, Regional Banks, Gold Miners…) — not every industry has a liquid ETF, so coverage is partial but every one listed has real mapped stocks behind it.
⚡ Quick & Saved filters

One-click presets (Equity, S&P 500). Build any search, hit ＋ Save to name and store it — saved filters persist in your browser and reappear as buttons.

🧹 Active filter chips & Export

Every active filter shows as a chip below the bar — click its ✕ to drop just that one, or Clear all to reset. The counter shows "X of 2,106 matched", and Export CSV downloads exactly the rows currently shown.
